How it works

This is a digital advent calendar for wellness. Each day, you can open a new holiday card, with a warm-up week to ease you in.

🧠 💪 ✨

Each card has three small things to try: one for your body, one for your mind and one for your soft lil soul. Do whichever you fancy, or all three.

Tick off what you've done and your tree lights up. One tick lights a bulb, and all three make it shine.

🎲

Every card also has a bonus little side quest. Won't affect your tree, but it will remind you that you have free will to do completely optional things for funsies.

📓

If you like, you can also write reflection notes in a digital wellness journal as you go, or not. It's private and only saved on your browser.

About this project

Hi, I’m Darcy.

The idea came from my colleague Katia (thank you!), who made a digital advent calendar for her and her family to do festive crafts. I wanted to make something just because: a little reason to look after ourselves – and each other.

I’ve always preferred small habits to big “new year, new me” resolutions, and hate the idea of waiting until January (or some arbitrary later date when your to-do list is done) to make progress towards your goals, especially when that goal is partly just to enjoy our silly little lives.

There’s no sign-up required and no data tracking. Everything stays on your browser.

This project is free to use, but if you’d like to give something back for using this tool, please donate to Samaritans, or pass a small kindness on to someone near you.
